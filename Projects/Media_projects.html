Rocketry Media Projects

I made the video above using the Blender. I had imported the CAD designs and animated all the pieces to come together, showcase its function, and how it fits into the overall launch vehicle. To do this, I had to create rigging for the components and animate their motion using inverse kinematics. I then edited the material of the individual pieces before creating the camera setup. After the video rendered, it was brought into Davinci Resolve to do the final logo transition.
